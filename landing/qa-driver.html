One panel. Every machine.

Carbon Panel runs Minecraft servers wherever you have machines — the PC in your room, a box in the closet, a VPS in the cloud — and manages them as one fleet behind one address.

  1. Players get one address.

    survival.yourdomain.com is all they ever type — one DNS record and one port, no juggling.

  2. The proxy routes every connection.

    The handshake names the destination, so Carbon Panel sends each player to the right container on the right machine.

  3. Servers run where you have machines.

    Keep the always-on survival at home and burst event servers into the cloud — or the other way around.

  4. One control plane keeps it in sync.

    Schedules, backups, routes and consoles work the same across every machine — and idle servers deep-sleep at zero RAM.
